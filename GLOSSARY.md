# Ledger

Ledger helps people understand the connected demo company's accounting records and review proposed changes.

## Performance reporting

**Profit and loss (P&L)**: Reported income, costs, and profit for a date range. Invoice totals alone are not a profit-and-loss report.

**Balance sheet**: Reported assets, liabilities, and equity at a particular date.

**Account movement**: The difference in a reported account's amount between two comparable periods or dates. It describes an observed change, not its business cause.

**Source row**: A row in a retrieved accounting report supporting a displayed amount or explanation.

**Performance explorer**: Ledger's workspace for inspecting financial reports, comparing them, and asking questions tied to source rows.
